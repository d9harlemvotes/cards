Marcus & His ID

A 14-year voter. A REAL ID that may not count. Get your documents in order now.

What the SAVE Act would change — and how to prepare.

THE SITUATION

Marcus is 34, lives in Harlem, has voted since 2010. He has a NY REAL ID driver’s license. No passport. Today he can update his registration online in 5 minutes when he moves apartments.

WHAT THE SAVE ACT WOULD DO

The SAVE America Act passed the U.S. House on February 11, 2026. It stalled in the Senate in April 2026 but its provisions are still being pursued. A standard REAL ID would not count as proof of citizenship. Any registration update — moving, marrying, switching parties — would require an in-person trip with a passport, certified birth certificate, or NY Enhanced License.

FOR MARCUS
Getting documents in order now protects his right to vote no matter what Washington does next. NY Enhanced License: $30 at the DMV. NY birth certificate: ~$30 at vitalrecords.health.ny.gov. Passport: $165, lasts 10 years.
