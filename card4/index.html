Naturalized "new" citizens

Fatou's Decision

A citizen since 2021. A baby on her back. A choice she is weighing on her own block.

When the right is real and the choice is still hard.

THE SITUATION

Fatou is 38, a hospital nurse, lives on West 116th Street with her husband and two young sons. She came from Senegal in 2012 and naturalized in 2021. She has every right to register and vote. She has not done so yet. Her hesitation isn’t about her status — she’s a citizen, full stop. It’s about what she’s seen in the news.

WHAT’S HAPPENING NOW

Federal agencies have demanded voter rolls from more than half the states. NY refused — and is fighting in federal court to keep its list private. Courts in Oregon, California, and Michigan have already sided with the states in the same fight. The federal Voting Rights Act and NY’s 2022 Voting Rights Act both protect her vote.

FOR FATOU
NY does not share its voter list with federal immigration agents. She’s a citizen — voting is her right. Whatever she decides today, the choice is hers. Take the card home and decide on your own time.
